## Objective
Improve a chart or visual data section so that users can understand the insight without relying only on the image, colour, or visual layout.

## Key Idea
A chart should not be the only source of information.

Users need:
- a clear heading
- a short insight summary
- accessible data table or list
- meaningful image alternative where appropriate
- no colour-only meaning

## What to Test

- [ ] Are abbreviations or units explained?
